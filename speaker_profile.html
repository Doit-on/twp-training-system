Professional Biography

Elaine Li is an Academic Services Consultant at National Geographic Learning, where she focuses on supporting the development of innovative and effective English language learning solutions. She earned her bachelor’s degree in English education from South China Normal University.

She has extensive experience as an English teacher and homeroom teacher in the international department of a school in Guangzhou. This experience in a multilingual environment deepened her understanding of student development. She is committed to creating meaningful learning experiences that help students discover their potential and support holistic growth.

Now, as an academic services consultant, she has trained thousands of teachers in China and other parts of Asia both face-to-face and online. She draws on her practical background to collaborate with educators, develop supportive content, and implement effective teaching practices.
